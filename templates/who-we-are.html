WHO WE ARE

OUR AMERICAN DREAM

The American Dream means something different to everyone. For some, it might be helping save lives in emergency situations. For others, it might be dedicating each and every day to sports. For us, we are honored to serve our Louisiana community through our delicious gourmet and stuffed burgers. With restaurants located in Sulphur, Moss Bluff, and Lake Charles, we get to do what we love every day, stuffing the burgers, tummies, and hearts of people we care about.

We started with a very humble beginning in Sulphur, Louisiana. Our vision then, and today, was to provide a higher standard of customer service while also serving the most delicious burgers in the state. We were personally tired of robotic customer service experiences where we left feeling like just another number passing through the corporate system. That is why Maplewood Burgers' primary focus is to provide a substantially different experience.

LOCALLY
OWNED

Our definition of the American Dream is to get to know our community on a first-name basis, while serving the most unique, mouth-watering, gourmet, and stuffed burgers in all of Louisiana.

Saay Patel, our owner, is an actively involved Board Member of the Boys Village Foundation. As a Board Member, Saay is passionately engaged in community events that aim to improve the lives and socioeconomic status of families and children in Louisiana.


Saay is also a Partner in Education at the R.W. Vincent Middle School in Sulphur, Louisiana.
